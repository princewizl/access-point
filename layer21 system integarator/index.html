E-Invoicing

Submit invoices to the government — automatically.

Nigeria now requires businesses to send invoices directly to the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS). Layer21 is an officially accredited Access Point Provider — meaning we're the secure bridge between your accounting system and the government.

You don't need to change how you work. We plug into what you already use and handle everything else.

  • Works with Odoo, SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, Sage, and more
  • Invoice validated and acknowledged in under 2 seconds
  • If there's a network issue, we automatically retry — no failed submissions
  • Full audit records and government receipts stored for 7 years
  • Test environment available before going live
